# SplatDuo — Support

SplatDuo is a game for iPhone: bugs crawl over a newspaper on your screen and you squash them with a finger. On iPhone Duo, closing the phone squashes them all at once.

## Questions

**Where is the menu?**
The top-left corner of the page is folded over. Tap it, or pull it with your finger: the page turns and the menu is printed underneath. Tap the page beside it to close it; it also closes by itself after a few seconds.

**How do I choose the bugs?**
In the menu, under "Bugs": All, Flies, Mosquitoes, Spiders, Roaches or Slugs. Under "Number", − and + set how many start the round, from 1 to 15.

**How does a round work?**
The bugs come out of the hole in the corner of the page. Every three you squash bring two more, until only three are left. The stopwatch starts with your first squash and stops with the last. Tap the page to play again.

**Is my best time saved?**
Not yet. Your time is shown at the end of each round.

**There is no sound.**
Open the menu and check that "Sound" is on (outlined in red), then check the volume of your iPhone.

**The sound plays although my iPhone is on silent.**
SplatDuo plays its sound even in Silent mode. To turn it off, open the menu and tap "Sound".

**Can I squash the bugs by closing the phone?**
Yes, on iPhone Duo: close it, and every bug on the half that folds over is squashed at once. On other iPhones, use your finger.

**How do I get a clean page?**
The stains dry, and when there are many the oldest fade away. For a fresh page right away, open the menu and pick a kind of bug (the same one works too).

**Which iPhones, which languages?**
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is in English, French and Dutch.
