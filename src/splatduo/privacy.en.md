# SplatDuo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

SplatDuo does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no account and asks for no personal information.
- The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
- The app saves three settings on your device only: the kind of bug, the number of bugs and whether sound is on. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
